    How long the edge lives

    Exit at the open. The overnight window is pre-registered and fixed. Every longer horizon is recalibrated and secondary, and by day five the interval on the mean includes zero. Rank correlation decays from 0.236 (p = 0.0003) to 0.058 (p = 0.380) over the same span, which rules out post-earnings drift as the mechanism. There is no version of this signal you hold for a week.

    What it misses when it stays quiet

    asymmetry_direction_confusion.csv · 268 scored documents, the diagnostic set, wider than the 233-event clean corpus. Do not read these rows against the 65.3% figure.

    The failure mode is abstention, not error. On events that actually rose, the model returned HOLD 34.7% of the time. On events that fell it returned HOLD 32.5% of the time. Those are the trades a desk never sees, and they do not appear anywhere in the 65.3% figure.

    Read alongside the size of the move: accuracy is 88% on moves above 10% and 57% on moves of 2% to 5%. The size of the move is not knowable at decision time, so that split cannot be traded on directly. It is a reason to weight a call, not to place one.

    Throughput, which is the actual product

    1 name40 names

    A reader averages 27.9 minutes per document. The model's median is 15.0 seconds, with a long tail to 127 seconds on the biggest filings. On a night when a dozen of your names report at once, the constraint is not judgement, it is reading time before the open. That is the gap the tool closes, and it is the only claim here that does not depend on the accuracy convention you pick.

    Levers tested and not shipped

    Conviction sizing is the one a desk asks for first. It was swept across 85 slope and cap combinations, and it wins in sample. It is not shipped: the gain sits inside the noise of a 44-trade book, it raises maximum drawdown from 37.4% to 41.4%, and the project's own rule is that no lever is promoted unless it clears in-sample, leave-one-out, permutation and deflated-Sharpe gates together. It cleared some. The book ships flat size.

    Method and limits

    A replay artefact, not a live product. It shows what the system produced at a past decision point, then reveals what happened, so the workflow can be judged before anyone trusts it with size.

    Deliberately absent

    Not shownWhy
    An equity curvePositions overlap, so any curve is order dependent and describes a book nobody could actually run. Mean net per trade is order independent, so that is what appears instead.
    A Sharpe ratioThe figure once reported as one was a t-statistic, which grows with sample size. It was retracted and is not quoted anywhere on this page.
    A confident recommendationThresholds were fitted in sample on a metric the study no longer reports. A decision layer that cannot be calibrated should not present itself as calibrated.
    A claim to beat the human armThe gap is 2.6pp against a detectable threshold of roughly 23pp at this sample. The honest statement is that the comparison is untestable here.
    An outcome at decision timeThe move has not happened yet. The reveal exists in replay only, behind an explicit step.

    Why the screen is built this way

    ChoiceThe finding behind it
    Blotter first, one row per eventA desk covering thirty names does not start from a single ticker. It starts from tonight's list and needs an ordering.
    Evidence folded away, every claim tied to its passageThe output that survives every caveat intact. Shown on demand because the call comes first and the audit comes second.
    Four short flags, not a paragraphAccuracy varies with move size and sector. That changes how much weight a call deserves, which is a sizing input, not prose.
    Disagreement shown, never scoredAgreement between the two arms is kappa 0.109. A filter built on agreement was retracted at −0.3pp, p = 0.959.
    A pending state before settlementThe outcome lands about fifteen hours later, so trust calibrates over weeks, not inside a session.
    Cost and latency on the call itselfThey are the two numbers that survive every convention argument in this study.

    The honest summary

    The system reads an earnings document in seconds and produces a score that ranks returns at rho 0.236, p = 0.0003, with no threshold applied. The decision layer built on top of it cannot be calibrated at 233 events. So the defensible product is a reading aid that gets a person to a judgement faster, with the evidence attached so the judgement can be checked. Not a system that makes the judgement.
