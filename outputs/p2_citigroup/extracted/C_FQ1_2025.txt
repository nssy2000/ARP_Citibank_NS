First Quarter Financial Results

Citigroup Citigroup revenues of $21.6 billion in the first quarter 2025 increased 3%(5), on a reported basis, driven by growth in each of Citi’s businesse s, largely offset by a decline in All Other. Excluding the divestiture-related impacts in both periods (6), revenues were also up 3%. Net interest income increased 4%, driven by U.S. Personal Banking (USPB), Markets, Wealth and Services, largely offset by declines in All Other and Banking. Non-interest revenue increased 1%, driven by Markets, Banking and Wealth, offset by declines in All Other, USPB and Services.
