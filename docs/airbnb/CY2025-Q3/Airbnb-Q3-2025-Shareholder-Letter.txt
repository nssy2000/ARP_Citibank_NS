Q3 2025 Shareholder Letter

Q3 2025 was another strong quarter for Airbnb. Revenue increased 10% year-over-year, landing at the high end of our guidance range. Adjusted EBITDA was over $2.0 billion, our highest in any quarter. Gross Booking Value increased 14% year-over-year, while Nights and Seats Booked rose by 9%. Both metrics accelerated from Q2 and exceeded our expectations, driven by strength in the U.S. market and ADR. We're driving continued growth by focusing on four key areas: making our service better, bringing Airbnb to more parts of the world, expanding what we offer, and integrating AI into our app.

Making Airbnb better: Reserve Now, Pay Later (US feature letting guests reserve an eligible stay and pay $0 upfront) helped drive the acceleration of Nights and Seats Booked in Q3, to be rolled out more broadly. Updated cancellation policies: hosts can choose a new policy allowing free cancellation up to 14 days before check-in; for stays under 28 nights, guests can cancel for a full refund up to 24 hours after booking confirmation if booked more than 7 days before check-in -- initial tests show this reduces customer service cases and increases annualized bookings. Improved maps and flexible search carousels also launched.

Bringing Airbnb to more parts of the world: average growth rate of nights booked on an origin basis in expansion markets continues to be twice that of core markets over the last 12 months. First-time bookers increased over 20% in Japan and nearly 50% in India YoY.

Expanding what we offer: since the May launch of Airbnb Services and reimagined Airbnb Experiences, feedback has been overwhelmingly positive (average rating 4.93/5). In Q3 2025, almost half of experiences bookings were not attached to an accommodations booking. Over 110,000 applications received from potential services/experiences hosts. New social features: "Who's going", direct messaging between guests who met on an experience, "Connections" in profile.

Integrating AI: smarter AI customer support (tailored answers, can take actions like canceling/changing dates directly from chat), expanding beyond the U.S. (already reduced need to contact a human agent by ~15%) starting with Spanish in Mexico, and building AI-powered conversational search.

Outlook
Q3 2025 results demonstrate continued focus on driving faster growth, with nights booked growth accelerating relative to Q2. In October, continued strong demand despite tougher YoY comparisons, with strength in longer lead-time bookings partly driven by Reserve Now, Pay Later.
- Q4 Revenue: expected $2.66B-$2.72B, YoY growth of 7%-10%, small FX tailwind. Implied take rate expected relatively flat YoY.
- Q4 GBV and Nights/Seats Booked: GBV expected to grow low-double-digits YoY (modest ADR increase from price appreciation/FX); Nights and Seats Booked expected mid-single-digit YoY growth due to the challenging Q4 2024 comparison.
- Full-Year Adjusted EBITDA: now expect ~35% margin for FY2025; Q4 2025 Adjusted EBITDA expected flat-to-down slightly YoY, margin declining, primarily due to investments in new growth and policy initiatives (~$200M into services/experiences in 2025). Looking to 2026: focused on maintaining strong margins while continuing to invest in growth initiatives.

Local investment highlights: fee-free installments up to 6 months in Brazil (accounted for nearly 50% of total bookings value in Q3); Chuseok holiday campaign in Korea (nights booked +67% YoY on origin basis); Kansai region social partnership in Japan (nights booked +30%+ YoY).

Q3 2025 Business and Financial Performance
Nights & Seats Booked: 133.6M (9% Y/Y, accelerating vs Q2). Excluding North America (~30% of total), growth was double-digit YoY. App bookings 62% of total nights booked (up from 58% prior year), app nights booked +17% YoY. GBV $22.9B (14% Y/Y, 12% ex-FX), ADR $171 (+5% YoY, +2% ex-FX).

Geographic Performance:
- North America: mid-single digit growth in Nights and Seats Booked YoY (sequential acceleration), driven by strong domestic travel and longer lead times; ADR +5% YoY (mix shift). Reserve Now, Pay Later launched in August helped drive acceleration.
- EMEA: mid-single digit growth YoY (slightly unfavorable comp vs 2024 Paris Olympics). ADR +10% YoY (+4% ex-FX).
- Latin America: low-20s growth YoY. ADR +4% YoY (+3% ex-FX). Brazil origin nights booked +20%+, first-time bookers +17% YoY; interest-free installment plans (up to 6 months) introduced in July drove higher bookings.
- Asia Pacific: mid-teens growth YoY. ADR +2% YoY (+3% ex-FX). Japan domestic travel nights booked +27% YoY (third consecutive quarter of acceleration); first-time bookers +20%+ in Japan, nearly 50% in India YoY.

Supply: active listings grew approximately in-line with Nights and Seats Booked, most growth in Latin America and Asia Pacific. Since 2023, removed over 550,000 low-quality listings; YoY reduction in customer service issue rates and chargeback rates. Nearly 500 million nights booked at Guest Favorite listings since launch.

Q3 2025 Financial Detail
Revenue $4.1B (10% Y/Y). Net Income $1.4B (34% margin) -- stable YoY as higher revenue was offset by growth/policy investments and a $213 million valuation allowance against deferred tax assets related to CAMT credits (net income margin 34% vs 37% in Q3 2024). Full-year 2025 effective tax rate expected ~21%. Starting 2026, long-term effective tax rate expected to decline to mid-to-high teens due to the One Big Beautiful Bill Act (OBBBA), enacted July 4. Adjusted EBITDA $2.1B (+5% YoY), 50% margin (vs 52% in Q3 2024).

Monetization and Take Rate: implied take rate (revenue/GBV) of 17.9%, down YoY from 18.6%, primarily due to FX and booking/stay timing. Guest travel insurance revenue (available in 12 largest countries) up over 25% YoY.

In October, simplified fee structure: migrated property management software (PMS) hosts on split fee structure (3% host fee + separate guest service fee) to a single 15.5% service fee; non-PMS hosts on single service fee structure also moved to 15.5%. Hosts can adjust prices to maintain same net earnings; guests continue to see full price upfront.

Shares: repurchased $857 million of Class A common stock during Q3 2025. Fully diluted share count reduced from 665M (Sept 2024) to 646M (Sept 2025).

Balance Sheet and Cash Flows: as of September 30, 2025, $11.7 billion of cash and cash equivalents, short-term investments, and restricted cash, plus $7.2 billion of funds held on behalf of guests. As of September 30, 2025, authorization to purchase up to $6.6 billion of Class A common stock remained under share repurchase programs. Since inception of repurchases in Q3 2022, fully diluted share count decreased ~8%; repurchases plus employee tax obligations total $12.4 billion.
