Q2 2025 Shareholder Letter

Airbnb had a strong Q2, exceeding expectations across key metrics including bookings, revenue, and margins. Despite global economic uncertainty early in the quarter, travel demand picked up, and nights booked on Airbnb accelerated from April to July. We continued executing on our multi-year strategy to perfect our core service, accelerate growth in global markets, and launch and scale new offerings.

Perfect our core service: We drove growth in Q2 by building on the hundreds of product improvements we've made over the past few years. During the quarter, we saw a lift in revenue from improvements across checkout, messaging, merchandising, and more flexible payment options. We're also expanding our AI-powered customer service agent to 100% of U.S. users, reducing the percentage of hosts and guests who needed to contact a human agent by 15%.

Accelerate growth in global markets: For six consecutive quarters, nights booked on an origin basis in our expansion markets have grown at about twice the rate of our core markets. Japan is one example: in Q2, nights booked by Japanese travelers accelerated from Q1, driven by more domestic travel and a 15% year-over-year increase in first-time bookers.

Launch and scale new offerings: In May, we expanded beyond stays with the launch of Airbnb Services and reimagined Airbnb Experiences. We also introduced an all-new Airbnb app, making it easier to book stays, experiences, and services in one place.

Q2 2025 Financial Results
- Q2 revenue was $3.1 billion, up 13% year-over-year, driven by solid growth in nights stayed, a slight increase in Average Daily Rate ("ADR"), and the timing of Easter.
- Q2 net income grew 16% Y/Y to $642 million, representing a 21% net income margin, primarily due to higher revenue.
- Q2 Adjusted EBITDA grew 17% Y/Y to $1.0 billion, representing a 34% Adjusted EBITDA Margin, primarily due to higher revenue.
- Q2 Free Cash Flow was $1.0 billion, representing a FCF Margin of 31%. TTM FCF was $4.3 billion, representing a TTM FCF Margin of 37%.
- Q2 share repurchases of $1.0 billion. As of June 30, 2025, we had $11.4 billion of cash and cash equivalents, short-term investments, and restricted cash, as well as $11.1 billion of funds held on behalf of guests. Today we're announcing a new share repurchase program with authorization to purchase up to an additional $6 billion of Class A common stock.

Outlook
Q2 2025 marked another strong quarter, with year-over-year growth of nights booked accelerating throughout the three months. As we look ahead to Q3, we're encouraged by current demand trends, though we expect a tougher year-over-year comparison toward the end of the quarter, continuing into Q4.
- Q3 Revenue: expected $4.02B-$4.10B, representing YoY growth of 8%-10%, minimal FX impact. Implied take rate expected flat YoY.
- Q3 Nights and Seats Booked and ADR: expected relatively stable vs. Q2 2025; ADR to increase modestly YoY, primarily driven by FX.
- Q3 Adjusted EBITDA: expected to increase to over $2.0 billion, though margin expected lower than Q3 2024 due to investments in new growth and policy initiatives; similar YoY decline expected in Q4 2025 margin.
- FY Adjusted EBITDA: full-year 2025 Adjusted EBITDA Margin expected at least 34.5%, including ~$200 million invested in services and experiences in 2025.

2025 Summer Release
On May 13, Airbnb expanded beyond places to stay: Airbnb Services, reimagined Airbnb Experiences, and an all-new redesigned app. This was Airbnb's largest launch to date. Feedback from guests who've booked services and experiences has been overwhelmingly positive, with an average rating of over 4.9 out of 5 stars since launch. More than 60,000 applications submitted from potential services/experiences hosts since launch.

Global Partnerships: announced a three-year partnership with the Tour De France, a first-ever live music partnership with Lollapalooza festivals worldwide, continued partnership with the International Olympic Committee for the upcoming Winter Olympics in Milan, and a major multi-tournament partnership with FIFA (FIFA Club World Cup 2025, FIFA World Cup 26 in North America, FIFA Women's World Cup 2027 in the Americas).

Q2 2025 Business and Financial Performance
Nights & Seats Booked: 134.4M (7% Y/Y). Gross Booking Value: $23.5B (11% Y/Y, 9% Y/Y ex-FX). Nights and Seats Booked grew double-digits YoY when excluding North America (~30% of total). Nights booked on app increased 17% YoY, accounting for 59% of total nights booked (up from 55% prior year). ADR was $174, up 3% YoY (1% ex-FX).

Geographic Performance:
- North America: low-single digit growth in Nights and Seats Booked YoY. ADR up 3% YoY, primarily mix shift. Improvement in travel demand following broader macro uncertainty earlier in the quarter; US nights booked (destination basis) accelerated each month through Q2, driven by domestic travel.
- EMEA: mid-single digit growth in Nights and Seats Booked YoY. ADR up 9% YoY (3% ex-FX). Germany (expansion market) saw double-digit YoY growth, an acceleration vs Q1.
- Latin America: high-teens growth in Nights and Seats Booked YoY. ADR down 3% YoY (up 2% ex-FX). Brazil drove meaningful growth, origin nights booked up high-teens rate, first-time bookers up ~20% YoY.
- Asia Pacific: mid-teens growth in Nights and Seats Booked YoY. ADR up 2% YoY (1% ex-FX). Japan: origin nights booked accelerated vs Q1, first-time bookers up 15% YoY.

Supply: Active listings grew at a healthy pace, slightly above Nights and Seats Booked growth, with most growth in Latin America and Asia Pacific. Co-Host Network supports over 100,000 listings (over 10 million nights booked since launch). Since 2023, removed over 500,000 low-quality listings. Nights booked at Superhost-managed listings increased 12% YoY.
