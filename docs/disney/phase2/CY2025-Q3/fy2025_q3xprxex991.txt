THE WALT DISNEY COMPANY REPORTS

THIRD QUARTER AND NINE MONTHS EARNINGS FOR FISCAL 2025

BURBANK, Calif. – The Walt Disney Company today reported earnings for its third fiscal quarter ended June 28, 2025.

Financial Results for the Quarter:

• Revenues increased 2% for Q3 to $23.7 billion from $23.2 billion in Q3 fiscal 2024

• Total segment operating income (1) increased 8% for Q3 to $4.6 billion from $4.2 billion in Q3 fiscal 2024

• Diluted earnings per share (EPS) for Q3 improved to $2.92 from $1.43 in Q3 fiscal 2024, and adjusted EPS (1) increased 16% for Q3 to $1.61 from $1.39 in Q3 fiscal 2024

Key Points:

• Entertainment: Segment operating income of $1.0 billion, a $179 million decrease versus Q3 fiscal 2024, driven by a difficult comparison to Q3 fiscal 2024's theatrical results (Inside Out 2) and higher marketing costs, partially offset by growth in Direct-to-Consumer

• Sports: Segment operating income of $1.0 billion, an increase of $235 million versus Q3 fiscal 2024, driven by ESPN

• Experiences: Revenue increased 8% to $9.09 billion, with domestic theme parks revenue up 10% to $6.4 billion; segment operating income grew behind higher guest spending and attendance at domestic parks, and the launch of Disney Cruise Line's newest ship

Guidance and Outlook:

• Fiscal Year 2025: Disney raised its full-year guidance and now expects adjusted EPS (1) of $5.85, an increase of 18% from fiscal 2024, versus prior guidance of 16% growth

• Experiences segment operating income growth guidance raised to 8% to 9% for fiscal 2025

• The Company continues to expect Entertainment segment operating income to grow double digits and Sports segment operating income to grow approximately 18% for fiscal 2025

Message From Our CEO:

"We delivered another quarter of strong results, with double-digit growth in adjusted EPS driven by outstanding performance across our Experiences and Sports businesses," said Robert A. Iger, Chief Executive Officer, The Walt Disney Company, on the continued momentum entering the final quarter of fiscal 2025.
